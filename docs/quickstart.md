# Quickstart: your first steps

This guide takes you from accessing the dashboard to launching a service.

## Before you start

You need an account that is authorized to use the platform. If you do not have access, see [Access the platform](access.md).

## Quick steps

1. Open the [SIESTA dashboard](https://dashboard.cloud.eosc-siesta.eu/).
2. Select **Login** and sign in using the method provided to you.
3. Open **Service catalog** and browse the catalogs or search for the service you need. Read its description before launching it.
4. Open the service card and select **Launch**. Complete the options shown in the form; these vary by service.
5. Check the service status and open your instance from **My Services**.
6. When you are finished, save any required results and stop or delete the service.

> Workflows vary by service. Do not enter personal or sensitive data until you have confirmed that the selected service and workspace are suitable for your use case.

## Where can I find my resources?

The dashboard menu links to **My Services**, **My Secrets**, **File Explorer**, and **Data Explorer**. Availability and behavior may depend on your account permissions and platform configuration.

- **My Services**: view services you have launched.
- **My Secrets**: manage secrets associated with your services, if you have access.
- **File Explorer** and **Data Explorer**: browse storage resources available in your account.

For more information, see [Explore the dashboard](dashboard.md) and the [service catalog](catalog/index.md).
