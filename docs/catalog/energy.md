# Energy forecasting catalog

The **Energy forecasting catalog** includes services for uploading local energy consumption or generation data and generating short-term forecasts, as well as tools for visualizing solar energy time series.

## Services available

### uc3-client-ui

This service allows users to upload a dataset with solar energy consumption or generation information and receive a forecast for the next 24 hours. It is designed to support quick, user-driven forecasting workflows for energy management and planning.

### uc3-researcher-ui

This service provides a browser-based interface for visualizing solar energy time series data from anonymized providers. It is intended for researchers and analysts who need to explore historical patterns and trends in solar generation or consumption data.

### uc3-energy-forecasting

This service is designed to predict household energy consumption and generation patterns in solar-equipped homes, with the goal of improving battery surplus optimization and supporting more efficient energy management decisions.
