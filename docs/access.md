# Access the platform

The main entry point for users is the [SIESTA dashboard](https://dashboard.cloud.eosc-siesta.eu/). The portal brings together tools and services intended to facilitate the secure sharing of sensitive data.

## Sign in

### First-time access

1. Open the [SIESTA dashboard](https://dashboard.cloud.eosc-siesta.eu/) and select **Login**.
2. On the sign-in page, choose the identity provider associated with your account. If you are accessing SIESTA through your institution and have EduGAIN credentials, select your institution when that option is available. Otherwise, use the provider linked to your account, such as GitHub, Google, IFCA SSO, or ORCID.
3. Sign in with that provider and complete any requested authentication or consent steps.
4. If prompted to register or request access, provide the requested details. Explain your purpose for joining SIESTA, your institution or organization, and which services you want to use.
5. Submit the form and wait for any required approval. Your available catalogs and services depend on your access group; see [Roles and permissions](roles.md).
6. When you return to the dashboard, open **Service catalog** and check that you can see the catalogs and services you need.

## Log in and check your access level

1. Open the [SIESTA dashboard](https://dashboard.cloud.eosc-siesta.eu/) and select **Login**.
2. Sign in with the same identity provider you used to register or that your administrator associated with your account.
3. Open **Service catalog**. If a catalog or service you expect is missing, contact your administrator or the support team to check your group membership and access level.

If you do not know which identity provider to use, or you need to request a higher access level, contact the support team. Include your institution, the service you need, and a brief explanation of your use case. Do not send passwords, tokens, or sensitive data.

## Common issues

- **I cannot sign in:** check that you are using the identity associated with your account and have completed the authentication steps.
- **I am signed in but cannot see a service or resource:** this may be related to your permissions. See [Roles and permissions](roles.md) and contact your administrator.
- **My session has expired:** sign in again from the dashboard.
