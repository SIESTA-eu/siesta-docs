# Security, good practices, and support

SIESTA includes a set of tools and services intended to facilitate the secure management of sensitive data. Review the official terms before using real data.

## Good practices

- Use only your own account and projects/workspaces you are authorized to access.
- Check the service, its inputs, and the selected workspace before uploading information.
- Do not include passwords, tokens, or other secrets in forms, files, or support messages.
- Keep only the data and results you need, and stop services when you no longer need them.
- Follow the [privacy policy](https://eosc-siesta.eu/privacy-policy/) and the [acceptable use policy and terms of use](https://eosc-siesta.eu/acceptable-use-policy-and-conditions-of-use/).

## Getting help

When reporting an issue to the SIESTA support team, include the service name, approximate time and error message. Do not attach sensitive data or credentials.
